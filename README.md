1.  **策略定义**：您只需输入目标市场、产品品类及关键词，系统将自动生成精准的客户画像与搜索矩阵。

**Core Product: NovaLeads**

[NovaLeads](https://www.novaxtra.com/leads/) is our AI-powered intelligent B2B sales lead generation platform. It is designed to address the core pain points faced by cross-border businesses in finding overseas customers, such as fragmented data, time-consuming verification, and low outreach efficiency.

**Core Workflow:**
1.  **Strategy Definition**: Input your target market, product category, and keywords. The system automatically generates precise customer profiles and a search matrix.
2.  **Intelligent Discovery & Verification**: The platform automatically discovers potential companies from multi-channel public data and verifies their authenticity through a proprietary aggregation engine.
3.  **Multi-Round Deep Qualification**: A multi-layer filtering model quickly eliminates invalid leads and conducts in-depth assessment based on business relevance to ensure high-quality matches.
4.  **360° Data Enrichment**: Automatically enriches target company profiles with key information, including contact emails, phone numbers, social media profiles, and business context, creating actionable lead cards.
5.  **Personalized Outreach Preparation**: AI generates personalized email drafts in the local language based on buyer context, provides Chinese translations, and features a collaborative review workflow to ensure communication quality and brand safety.
6.  **End-to-End Traceability**: The source of every lead is traceable, and all operations are logged, facilitating team collaboration management and compliance auditing.

**Core Value:**
*   **Boost Efficiency**: Frees sales teams from tedious, repetitive lead searching and cleansing tasks.
*   **Enhance Quality**: Delivers high-intent, actionable sales leads through intelligent verification and deep matching.
*   **Optimize Process**: Standardizes and streamlines personalized outreach preparation and collaborative review, accelerating the journey from lead to inquiry.
*   **Ensure Control & Compliance**: Provides full-process data traceability and auditability, helping businesses build a compliant and reliable overseas sales development system.
